## OBSERVATIONS

1. XGBoost achieved the best overall Macro F1 score.
2. LightGBM achieved similar performance with significantly lower training time.
3. CatBoost performed slightly worse due to extreme class imbalance.
4. DOM-based XSS detection remains the most challenging due to extremely small sample size.
5. Ensemble methods (LCCDE) are expected to further improve minority class detection.
