SECTION 5: RANKINGS & WINNER ANALYSIS
------------------------------------------------------------

🏆 RANK 1 — WINNER: Stacking (Stacked Generalisation)
  Macro F1        : 95.30%  (+0.09 pp over LCCDE)
  Accuracy        : 99.69%  (Highest accuracy on TF-IDF test set)
  Stored XSS Rec. : 0.99    (Highest recall among all methods)
  Inference Time  : 0.41 s  (2.5x faster inference than LCCDE)
  Why Rank 1      : In high-dimensional spaces like TF-IDF (5282 features), base
                    models rarely err. Stacking's OOF meta-learner successfully
                    learned fine-grained probability calibration across models,
                    boosting recall on Stored XSS from 0.97 to 0.99 and achieving
                    the highest overall accuracy and macro-F1 score.

🥈 RANK 2 — BEST NOVEL METHOD: CCDS (Confidence-Calibrated Dynamic Selection)
  Macro F1        : 95.22%  (+0.01 pp over LCCDE)
  Stored XSS Rec. : 0.98    (+1 pp over LCCDE)
  Why Rank 2      : CCDS out-performs LCCDE in both F1 and recall without requiring
                    any meta-training. By evaluating Shannon entropy and Jensen-
                    Shannon Divergence, it effectively identifies mild probability
                    disagreements on boundary Stored XSS attacks, soft-voting to
                    recover false negatives that LCCDE's hard-vote discards.

🥉 RANK 3 (TIED) — PCER (Per-Class Expert Routing) & WSV (Weighted Soft Voting)
  Macro F1        : 95.21%  (Tied with LCCDE, but +0.02 pp higher Macro Precision)
  Inference Time  : PCER is 0.31 s (3.4x faster than LCCDE!)
  Why Rank 3      : Both methods beat LCCDE on Macro Precision (94.10% vs 94.08%)
                    while executing far faster. PCER is the ultimate lightweight
                    champion: by consulting its pre-computed training precision
                    table, it performs zero complex decision logic at runtime,
                    completing 3,276 inferences in just 314 milliseconds.

RANK 5 — LCCDE (Baseline)
  Macro F1        : 95.21%
  Inference Time  : 1.06 s
  Why Rank 5      : Because TF-IDF yields highly separable feature vectors, base
                    models agree on 99.6% of samples. LCCDE performs reasonably
                    well here, but its sequential 3-tier rules make it over 3
                    times slower than PCER, and its hard-voting ignores soft
                    probabilistic cues captured by Stacking and CCDS.

RANK 6 — BMA (Bayesian Model Averaging)
  Macro F1        : 95.12%  (-0.09 pp below LCCDE)
  Why Rank 6      : Because LightGBM and XGBoost attain virtually identical, near-
                    perfect per-class training precisions on TF-IDF, BMA's weights
                    converge to uniform soft voting. This dilutes confident expert
                    predictions on difficult edge cases, slightly lowering precision
                    on Stored and DOM-based XSS attacks.

------------------------------------------------------------
WHY TF-IDF vs CHAR-CNN RESULTS DIFFER SO DRAMATICALLY:
------------------------------------------------------------
1. Dimensionality & Consensus:
   TF-IDF has 5,282 sparse vocabulary features; base models easily isolate linear
   decision boundaries, leading to high consensus. LCCDE performs adequately when
   models agree. In contrast, CharCNN uses a dense 410-dim embedding with much
   more model disagreement, exposing LCCDE's fatal flaw (0.13 DOM-based F1 vs
   CCDS's 0.89 DOM-based F1).

2. Novel Algorithm Value Proposition:
   - For complex, lower-dim representations with model conflict (CharCNN):
     CCDS is the clear champion, improving baseline Macro F1 by +20.08 pp!
   - For ultra-high dimensional representations where speed is critical (TF-IDF):
     PCER offers the best efficiency, cutting LCCDE runtime by over 70% while
     improving overall macro precision.
   - When absolute maximum accuracy is paramount regardless of training cost:
     Stacking extracts the highest accuracy (99.69%) and F1 (95.30%) on TF-IDF.
